My name is Jared, I am currently in my 2nd year of my PhD at McGill! I grew up in Vancouver, Canada where I completed my Bachelor's degree in Astronomy at the University of British Columbia in 2019.

From there, I went on to complete a Master of Data Science degree also at the University of British Columbia in 2021. I have since moved to Montreal and have come back into the astronomy community.

I am proficient in Python, R, and MATLAB, with experience in Data Science topics such as machine learning, data structures, workflow, statistics, and visualization. My research involves working with James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) time-series observations including data reduction and applying exoplanet spectral retrieval codes to study planetary atmospheres.
