Trottier Space Institute, McGill University

Hello! I'm a PhD Student at the Trottier Space Institute at McGill University. I use space-based telescopes to observe short-period, hot exoplanets. I am interested in the characterization of exoplanet atmospheres including their chemical composition, energy balance, and cloud coverage.
